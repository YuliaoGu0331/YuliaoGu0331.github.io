My research focuses on understanding the mechanical behavior and failure mechanisms of advanced materials under extreme conditions. 

### Molecular Dynamics Simulations of High-Entropy Alloys
Investigating the mechanical properties, defect evolution, and dislocation dynamics in FCC metals and high-entropy alloys (e.g., CoCrFeMnNi Cantor alloy) using LAMMPS. 

### Shock Physics and Material Failure
Studying the microstructural response of materials subjected to shock compression, with a specific emphasis on spallation phenomena and dislocation theories.

### Advanced Data Visualization
Employing computational tools and OVITO to analyze large-scale simulation results, identifying key microstructural features such as stacking faults and atomic displacements.
