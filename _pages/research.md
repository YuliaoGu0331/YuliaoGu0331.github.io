# Research

My academic interests are rooted in **solid mechanics**, with particular attention to how materials and structures deform, fail, and respond to external loading. I am currently developing the theoretical and computational foundation needed for further research in this area.

## Areas of interest

- Mechanics of materials and structures
- Computational methods in mechanics
- Multiscale descriptions of material behavior
