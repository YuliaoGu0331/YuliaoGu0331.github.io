# [Ph.D. in PKU]-5

{% include published-date.html date=page.date %}

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">phd5 和 phd4 之间隔了挺久，呵呵。这段时间确实没有什么很强的写作动力和写作时间。写作动力不足肯定是第一位的，每天上完课、开组会、看看看书，感觉上精力明显不够了，游戏都玩不进去，更何谈写作。至于写作时间这一项，我得承认这是我找理由了，实际上挤一挤应该有，但是我更愿意把这时间刷刷手机或者走走散散步。</p>
  <p lang="en">It has been quite a while between phd4 and phd5, haha. Lately I have not had much motivation or time to write. The lack of motivation is certainly the main reason. After classes, group meetings, and some reading each day, I can feel my energy running out. I can barely get into a game, let alone write. As for not having time, I have to admit that is an excuse. I could probably make room for it, but I would rather spend that time scrolling on my phone or going for a walk.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">相比于 phd4 的负能量，可以说我算是逐渐找到节奏和正轨了吧。在和同门还有老师的交流下，我发现现在越是急躁，反而越是做不出来什么东西。根本原因还是知识不够，整体的视野不够，所以对很多现象没什么本质的理解。随着阅读量的增加，我愈发发觉这一点至关重要。现在很多老师上来就让同学干，但缺乏物理和理论层面的认识，实际上最后技术挺熟练，但认知层面很低级。</p>
  <p lang="en">Compared with the negativity of phd4, I suppose I am gradually finding my rhythm and getting back on track. Conversations with fellow group members and teachers have made me realize that the more impatient I get, the less I manage to accomplish. At root, I still lack knowledge and a broad enough perspective, so I do not understand many phenomena at a fundamental level. The more I read, the more essential this seems. Many teachers now ask students to start doing the work straight away, without first building an understanding of the physics and theory. In the end, the students may become technically skilled while their conceptual understanding remains shallow.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">早上看完量子力学的相关内容后，我茅塞顿开。量子力学为我打开了微观的视角，我有一种之前学的都是啥啊的这种感受。很多科研都是立足于最基本的认识再上去深化，但是背后的物理始终不会改变，基于物理的原则出发，我们能够保证我们做的东西至少在物理上是说的通的，而不是只是唯象。</p>
  <p lang="en">After reading about quantum mechanics this morning, something suddenly clicked. It opened a microscopic perspective for me and made me wonder what, exactly, I had been learning before. Much research builds on the most basic understanding and then goes deeper, but the underlying physics does not change. Starting from physical principles lets us make sure that what we do at least makes physical sense, rather than being merely phenomenological.</p>
</div>

  <p lang="en">Life outside my studies has settled down too. My workspace is set up, and I now have an electric scooter to get around.</p>
