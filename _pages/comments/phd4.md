# [Ph.D. in PKU]-4

{% include published-date.html date=page.date %}

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">今天开完了开学典礼。整个人不知道是因为没睡醒，还是因为没有归属感，总感觉游离于人群之外。本来算是挺高兴的事，但我却高兴不起来。</p>
  <p lang="en">Today I made it through the opening ceremony. I could not tell whether I was simply not fully awake or lacked any sense of belonging, but I kept feeling detached from the crowd. It should have been a happy occasion, yet I could not feel happy.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">直博是不是错误的，我现在不能肯定，但就现在来说，我觉得应该是错误的。我没有什么科研的方法论，实在是太迷茫了，根本不知道做什么，也没有什么确定的前景。事实上从被北大录取那刻，我就有点后悔了，只是那时被名校录取的亢奋暂时蒙蔽了。我没有太多的科研兴趣，我也不是什么真正的天才，我确实能够学好一些东西，但是那时因为这些知识都是确定的，我能看的见未来。</p>
  <p lang="en">Was choosing the direct-entry Ph.D. track a mistake? I cannot say for certain yet, but as things stand, I think it probably was. I have no real methodology for doing research. I feel profoundly lost, with no idea what to do and no clear prospects ahead. In fact, I began to regret it from the moment I was admitted to PKU; the exhilaration of being accepted by a prestigious university simply obscured that regret for a while. I do not have much interest in research, nor am I any kind of true genius. I can certainly learn some things well, but that was because the knowledge was settled and I could see where it would lead.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">但现在我看不见。下午去对课题进行了些调研，我强迫我自己做出些选择，可什么也做不到。和当时我一心想选数学专业一样，我这个人啊，只是享受我在数学上的一点点天赋，享受做题做出带来的快感，而不是真心喜欢数学。现在也是差不多吧，我只是想找个我能做出来的课题，而没有真正喜欢的课题。这样真的会有好结果吗？</p>
  <p lang="en">But now I cannot see the future. This afternoon, I did some research into possible topics and forced myself to make some choices, but I could not manage any of it. It is much like the time when I was determined to major in mathematics. The truth is, I merely enjoyed the small amount of talent I had for math and the satisfaction of solving a problem; I did not genuinely love mathematics. Perhaps this is much the same. I am simply trying to find a topic I am capable of carrying through, rather than one I truly care about. Can that really lead to a good outcome?</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">我会怀疑自己，将来也会一直怀疑自己，但我永远不会到自杀的地步。现在的我已经释怀了，最差的地步，也只是退学，需要面对社会、家人、朋友的审视。我能做的只有尽我所能，然后接受所要发生的结果。</p>
  <p lang="en">I will doubt myself, and I probably always will, but I will never reach the point of suicide. I have already made peace with things. The worst that could happen is simply leaving the program and facing the judgment of society, my family, and my friends. All I can do is give it everything I have, and then accept whatever result comes.</p>
</div>
