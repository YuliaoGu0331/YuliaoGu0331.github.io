# [Ph.D. in PKU]-3

{% include published-date.html date=page.date %}

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">今日是最累的一天，虽然说都是形式的灌水讲座，但是人既然去了，也多少得听一些，而这一听，便是几个小时。</p>
  <p lang="en">Today was the most exhausting day so far. Although the talks were largely perfunctory formalities, once I was there, I still had to listen to at least some of them—and that meant several hours.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">讲座会议的主要内容是关于学生工作的，学生工作的招新其实对我来说还是颇有吸引力的，本科期间也陆陆续续参加了一些相关的活动，也想锻炼自己，认识认识新朋友。一想到学生工作可能会占用时间，还需要过问导师的意见，我就没有立马填写。</p>
  <p lang="en">The talks and meetings focused mainly on student organizations. Their recruitment was actually quite appealing to me: as an undergraduate, I took part in related activities from time to time, and I would still like to challenge myself and meet new people. But once I considered how much time this kind of work might take—and that I would need to ask my advisor's opinion—I decided not to fill out the application immediately.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">上午的讲座结束后火急火燎地吃完饭，立马就去和导师聊天了。我的情况有些特殊，选题也不是小导能够自己决定的，还需要我和大导讨论。由于行政与学习分别在两个组，因此我生来就需要承担比一般的同学更重的沟通与学习压力（组会等）。我自己喜欢偏AI一点的方向，无他，只是比较好玩，当然也有就业的因素，但最终方向我寻思大概不会是我心中所想。</p>
  <p lang="en">After the morning talks, I hurried through lunch and immediately went to speak with my advisor. My situation is somewhat unusual: my research topic is not something my immediate supervisor can decide alone, so I still need to discuss it with my principal supervisor. Because my administrative affiliation and academic work belong to two different groups, from the outset I will have to shoulder more communication and academic pressure than most students—group meetings and so on. I am personally drawn to directions with more AI in them, for no grand reason other than that they are more interesting. Employment prospects are part of it too, of course, but I suspect the final direction will probably not be what I have in mind.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">下午和晚上又是经典的会议。结束会议后，和我爸的朋友也是教授交流了一番，我大概是懂了博士的节奏与方向。剩下的，就只是沟通与协调了。</p>
  <p lang="en">The afternoon and evening brought another familiar round of meetings. After they ended, I spoke with one of my father's friends, who is also a professor. I think I now have a rough understanding of the rhythm and direction of a Ph.D. What remains is simply communication and coordination.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">另外，明天我一定得要到个工位，不然来回距离太过于遥远了。从复旦南区到光华楼，与畅春到新奥，我感觉后者更胜一筹。</p>
  <p lang="en">Also, tomorrow I absolutely have to secure a desk; otherwise, the journey back and forth is simply too long. Comparing the trip from Fudan's South Campus to the Guanghua Towers with the one from Changchun to the Xinao Engineering Building, I think the latter wins hands down.</p>
</div>
