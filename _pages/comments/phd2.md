# [Ph.D. in PKU]-2

{% include published-date.html date=page.date %}

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">今天终于是完成报道了，怎么说呢，报道也是稀松平常地走个过场，机械地完成流程。完成后我们便前往了寝室进行整理。</p>
  <p lang="en">Today I finally completed registration. How should I put it? Registration itself was little more than a routine formality, a process carried out almost mechanically. Once it was done, we headed to the dormitory to get everything in order.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">今天的寝室整理我可谓是几乎没出力，因此我不知道我有没有资格评论。我的父母确实一直在忙碌着，但整理的效率总体来说不高。如果是我自己来的话，想必会更快一点（根据我疫情那两年自己整理的经验），但相对的，我也会更累一些，所以也没必要抱怨什么。</p>
  <p lang="en">I did almost none of the work of organizing the dorm today, so I am not sure I am in any position to comment. My parents were certainly busy the whole time, but overall the process was not especially efficient. If I had come alone, I imagine I would have finished more quickly—judging from my experience of handling everything myself during the two pandemic years. Then again, I would also have been more tired, so there is no real reason to complain.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">我妈非常兴奋，总是问这问那，刚开始我还能勉强回应几句，可是后面我也无力了，因为我属实也是不熟悉北大的校园，我也是第二次来。我因此也有些心生烦闷了，现在回想起来总觉得对不起他们。</p>
  <p lang="en">My mother was extremely excited and kept asking about one thing after another. At first I could just about answer a few questions, but eventually I ran out of energy. After all, I am unfamiliar with the PKU campus myself—this was only my second visit. I began to feel a little irritated, and looking back now, I cannot help feeling guilty about how I treated my parents.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">唉，能这样和父母在一起走路、观光的时间又有多少呢，我后悔我之前的行为了。</p>
  <p lang="en">Sigh—how many more chances will I have to walk around and sightsee with my parents like this? I regret how I behaved earlier.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">晚饭依然是我爸的朋友招待，我不太喜欢这种类型的火锅。吃饭的时候和以前复旦的同学聊天，也是感到读书的无力感。这也是更加坚定我不读死书的观念，还是得以赚钱为核心。以成绩、科研、论文来定义人的观念，是完全的学生思维，太理想化了。</p>
  <p lang="en">Dinner was once again hosted by my father's friend. I am not particularly fond of this kind of hotpot. While eating, I chatted with former classmates from Fudan and again felt the sense of powerlessness that can come with studying. It strengthened my conviction that I should not bury myself in books; making money still has to remain at the core. Judging people by their grades, research, and papers is pure student thinking—far too idealistic.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">明天要和导师会面，同时要惯例性地开一些灌水的会议/新生教育，还是早点休息吧。第一个在北大的夜晚，还需要我适应。不得不说，北大今年来相比于复旦、清华，在硬件上、生活基础设施的配置上，已经远远落后了。生活、交通的不便，是我在北大求学过程中需要去想方设法解决的问题。</p>
  <p lang="en">Tomorrow I am meeting my advisor. There will also, as usual, be some perfunctory meetings and orientation sessions for new students, so I had better get some rest. I still need time to adjust to my first night at PKU. I have to say that, compared with Fudan and Tsinghua, PKU is now far behind in its facilities and basic living infrastructure. The inconveniences of daily life and transportation are problems I will need to find ways to solve during my time here.</p>
</div>
