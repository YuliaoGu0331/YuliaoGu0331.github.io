# Reflections on Fundamentals of Continuum Mechanics

{% include published-date.html date=page.date %}

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">终于把《连续介质力学基础》这本书看完了。感觉看这本书花的时间和之前读 Phillips 那本七八百页的书差不多，甚至还要更长，至少有二十几天，具体时间已经无法考证了。</p>
  <p lang="en">I have finally finished <em>Fundamentals of Continuum Mechanics</em>. It feels as though it took about as long as the seven- or eight-hundred-page book by Phillips that I read earlier—perhaps even longer. It took at least twenty days, though I can no longer recall the exact amount of time.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">总体而言，黄筑平先生的这本《连续介质力学基础》作为一本专业书籍，我觉得还是可以的。但要说它是一本优秀的教学类书籍，我个人认为还有差距。说难听一点，这本书还是有些“防自学”了；或者说，与以前的北大学生相比，我们的水平太差了。</p>
  <p lang="en">Overall, I think Huang Zhuping's <em>Fundamentals of Continuum Mechanics</em> works reasonably well as a specialist book. But I would not go so far as to call it an excellent textbook for teaching. To put it bluntly, it is not particularly friendly to self-learners—or perhaps we are simply much weaker than PKU students of earlier generations.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">这本书最大的特点是几乎没有什么图，也缺少对物理直觉和物理思想的解释，数学味道太浓了。前五章我认为还可以，从第六章到最后就开始不对劲了：上来就是一味推公式，而且很多地方需要很强的注意力和技巧。（PS：是不是黄筑平先生觉得后面的部分不是那么基础，看的人相对较少，所以写得更加深入了一点？）最后，我实在看得受不了了，最后几章也看得比较草。感受是每个公式在数学上的推导过程都能看懂，但对它们的物理含义却有点迷糊。用我同学 Kaiwei Wu 的话说，这很“苏式”。</p>
  <p lang="en">The book's most distinctive feature is that it contains almost no figures and offers little explanation of physical intuition or physical ideas; the mathematical flavor is overwhelming. The first five chapters seemed fine to me, but from Chapter Six onward something felt off: it plunges straight into formula derivations, many of which demand intense concentration and considerable technical skill. (P.S. Did Huang feel that the later material was less fundamental and would attract fewer readers, and therefore decide to treat it in greater depth?) By the end, I could hardly stand it anymore and read the last few chapters rather cursorily. I could follow the mathematical steps in each formula, but their physical meaning remained hazy. In the words of my classmate Kaiwei Wu, it feels very “Soviet-style.”</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">很多时候，看完后我都想问自己一句：到底学到了什么？我好像具体也说不出个所以然，很没有底气。昨天上 Mohan Chen 老师的微观分子模拟课时，老师说，学习的阶段是“不知道自己不知道——知道自己不知道——知道自己知道——不知道自己知道”。我希望自己最好能到最后一个阶段。</p>
  <p lang="en">More often than not, after finishing a section, I want to ask myself: what did I actually learn? I cannot seem to give a concrete answer, and I feel far from confident. In Professor Mohan Chen's microscopic molecular simulation class yesterday, he described the stages of learning as “you don't know what you don't know—you know what you don't know—you know what you know—you don't know what you know.” I hope I can eventually reach that final stage.</p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-passage">
  <p lang="zh-CN">顺便一提，虽然这篇不算在 Ph.D. 主线栏目内，但我还是想说一下近况。最近，负责带我的师兄终于让我放开做了，具体的联系与故事可以以后再讲。后续我的计划可能是把调研金刚石和学习一些具体基础知识的时间五五开，不会再像以前一样全力 all in 了。最近我的心态稍微好了一点，思路也算是逐渐清晰了，但具体要研究什么问题，还得再思考一下。</p>
  <p lang="en">On a related note, although this post is not part of the Ph.D. series, I would like to say a little about how things have been going. The senior student supervising me has finally given me the freedom to work independently; the details and the story behind that can wait for another time. Going forward, I plan to split my time roughly evenly between investigating diamond and learning specific foundational topics, rather than going all in on one thing as I did before. My state of mind has improved a little recently, and my thoughts are gradually becoming clearer, but I still need to think carefully about the exact problem I want to work on.</p>
</div>
